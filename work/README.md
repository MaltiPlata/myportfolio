# Where uploads go

Drop files in `uploads/`. I move them into `work/` with clean names and add them to the grid in `Portfolio v3.dc.html`.

## Naming
`<client>-<project>-<nn>.jpg` — e.g. `ugg-treadlite-01.jpg`, `dish-goldenglobes-03.jpg`

## Categories (filter chips on the site)
- Commercial
- Social
- Print & Digital
- Production Art
- Logos & Identity — appears once files exist
- Presentations — appears once files exist

Empty categories are hidden automatically.

## Sensitive material
Flag anything under NDA or with client-confidential numbers when you upload it. Options, cheapest to most work:
1. **Crop** — show the layout, cut the data.
2. **Redact bars** — solid accent/black bars burned into the image over names and figures.
3. **Blur** — soft blur on the sensitive region.
4. **Relabel** — swap real client name for "National retail brand", keep visuals.
5. **Gated** — thumbnail only, full deck on request.

Projects marked `nda: true` in the project list get a "NDA · REDACTED" badge on the card.
