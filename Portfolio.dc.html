About Me

I'm an adventure seeker for fun, and a designer by day.

With over 10 years of experience in the creative field, I've designed and conceptualized multi-million dollar ad campaigns across a wide range of mediums, industries, and audiences. I've had the privilege of collaborating with high-profile talent, including Tom Brady for UGG Australia and Kellee Edwards from HGTV's Design on a Dime for Ford's 2019 Explorer campaign.

My work has been showcased on major platforms like Times Square, Warner Media, ViacomCBS, UGG storefronts, Essence magazine, and billboards across New York, Los Angeles, and Boston.
